## Why Rocky?

Most AI assistants live in a browser tab.

Rocky lives with you.

It sits on your desktop like a tiny magical rock golem, listens when you talk, reacts with animations, remembers small things about you, and helps with everyday moments without feeling like another boring chatbot window.

> Rocky is not just an assistant.  
> Rocky is a little companion for your screen.

## Reminders And Alarms

Rocky can help you remember things without leaving your desktop.

```text

Set an alarm for 6:30 AM.

```

If Rocky is asleep when a reminder is due, it wakes up and tells you the reminder first.

Then, if it feels appropriate, Rocky celebrates after speaking.

## Privacy

Rocky tries to make important privacy moments clear.

- Screen sharing is off by default.
- Rocky shows a visible indicator whenever screen sharing is active.
- You can stop screen sharing at any time by saying `stop seeing my screen`.
- Your Gemini API key is stored locally in encrypted form on Windows.
- Personal memory is stored locally on your device.
- Voice interaction requires sending audio to Gemini.
- Weather and approximate location features may use network-based lookup.
- News search uses online news results.

Avoid showing passwords, private documents, financial information, or sensitive content while screen sharing is active.
